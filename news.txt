# PSVitaAlive 02.20 is available!

Manage multiple downloads and keep browsing while PSVitaAlive installs your applications.

## Download Manager
- Queue multiple apps and Install All groups.
- See icons, current progress, pending jobs and persistent history.
- Pause, resume or cancel supported operations.
- Explicit recovery of eligible pending jobs after restarting.

## Interface & Performance
- Animated queue feedback and reordering.
- D-pad and left-stick navigation in the download manager.
- START cycles cards, footer and header.
- Improved OLED protector with battery and queue information.
- Background icon decoding and fewer storage probes for smoother browsing during extraction.

## Get Version 02.20
Open PSVitaAlive with automatic updates enabled, or install the official PSVitaAlive.vpk over your existing app.
