# PSVitaAlive 02.17 is available!

The latest stable release makes the store cleaner, improves PSP and PS1 installation feedback, and fixes visual issues reported by the community.

---

## A Cleaner Store

- App and game cards now show the version without the extra release date. You can still find the date in each item's details.
- The filter button says **Filters** when no filter is selected.
- With a filter active, its name appears on the button and scrolls if needed.
- App sizes, statuses, installation badges, and Game/Data Files tags are still displayed.

## PSP & PS1 Improvements

- See the destination path while installing a PSP or PS1 game and again when installation completes.
- Installed badges show the detected storage device, such as **Installed ux0** or **Installed uma0**.
- The scanner also checks compatible game locations on imc0 and xmc0 when available.
- Clearer installation results and a better refresh after installing games.

## Stability & Performance

- Fixed screen flicker after completing an app update.
- Reduced unnecessary screen redraws and operations during PSP/PS1 file copies.

## Discord & Feedback

- Updated the Discord invitation and in-app QR code.
- Problem reports and suggestions can now go to their respective Discord forum threads.

Discord: https://discord.gg/byn8FDuDrw
